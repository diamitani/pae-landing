What is PAE?

Sales Outreach That Runs on Autopilot

PAE is a complete system for automating your sales emails safely. Upload 10 or 10,000 leads, and the system checks your CRM, finds email addresses, writes personalized messages, and sends them—all without manual work.

🛡️

Never Contact the Same Person Twice

Before reaching out to anyone, the system checks your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.) to see if you've already contacted them. This prevents awkward duplicate emails and keeps your sales team organized.

  • Checks HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, or Pipedrive
  • Finds existing conversations and deals
  • Stops before sending duplicate messages
🤖

Smart Automation + AI Research

The system uses reliable automation for checking contacts and sending emails. AI is only used for researching companies and writing personalized messages—so you get accuracy where it matters and personalization where it counts.

  • Automation handles: checking contacts, finding emails, sending
  • AI handles: company research and writing custom emails
  • Works with Claude, GPT-4, and DeepSeek
✅

Approve Before Sending

Get a notification in Slack showing who will receive emails and what the message says. Click to approve or reject before anything goes out—you're always in control.

  • Preview emails in Slack before they're sent
  • See who's getting contacted and why
  • One-click approve or reject for each batch
📊

See What's Happening

When something goes wrong, you'll know exactly what happened and how to fix it. The system tracks every step and explains errors in plain English.

  • Tracks each step of the process
  • Shows exactly where errors happen
  • Suggests how to fix problems
How It Works

Your 5-Step Outreach Process

Here's exactly what happens when you upload leads. Each step makes sure you're reaching the right people with the right message—safely.

📥

1. Upload Your Leads

Start by uploading a spreadsheet of companies or people you want to reach. You can also connect directly to your CRM to pull new leads automatically.

  • Upload a CSV file or spreadsheet
  • Connect your CRM to add leads automatically
  • Works with real-time lead alerts too
  • Run once or schedule to repeat daily
🛡️

2. Check Who You've Already Contacted

Before reaching out, the system checks your CRM to see if this person or company is already in your pipeline, has an open deal, or is already a customer.

  • Searches for existing deals and conversations
  • Checks if they're already a customer
  • Stops if the company is in your system
  • Prevents team members from doubling up
🔍

3. Find Contact Information

Get verified email addresses for the right people at each company. Works with tools like Clay, Apollo, ZoomInfo, and others to find the decision-makers you need.

  • Finds people by job title (e.g., "VP of Sales")
  • Verifies email addresses are real
  • Gets phone numbers and company details
  • Tries multiple sources to find the best data
✍️

4. Write Personalized Emails

AI researches each company and writes a custom 3-sentence email that mentions their specific situation. Every email sounds like you wrote it yourself.

  • 3 sentences: problem, why it matters, how you can help
  • Mentions specific details about their company
  • Uses Claude or GPT-4 to write naturally
  • Reusable templates for your product/service
📧

5. Send Emails Automatically

Approved contacts are added to your email sequence tool and sent automatically over the next few days. Works with Smartlead, Instantly, Lemlist, and HubSpot.

  • Connects to your email sending tool
  • Adds personalized details to each email
  • Tracks who received emails in your CRM
  • Handles unsubscribes automatically
Behind The Scenes

The 9 Steps That Make It Work

Here's what actually happens behind the scenes. Don't worry—you don't need to build this yourself. Just import the template and customize it for your needs.

01
Step 1

Start the Process

Starts when you upload a file, add leads to your CRM, or when new website visitors arrive.

Webhook or scheduled trigger

How It Works

The workflow can start three ways: (1) You upload a CSV file of leads, (2) It runs automatically on a schedule to pull from your CRM, or (3) It triggers when someone visits your website (if you use visitor tracking tools).

02
Step 2

Clean Up the Data

Fixes messy data—removes extra spaces, standardizes company names, formats job titles.

Data cleanup

How It Works

This step cleans up the information you uploaded: removes extra spaces, makes sure company websites are formatted correctly, standardizes job titles, and checks that all required information is there before moving forward.

03
Step 3

Check Your CRM

Searches your CRM for this person or company. Stops here if you've already contacted them.

HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, or Pipedrive

How It Works

Connects to your CRM and searches by company name and email address. It checks for: (1) Open deals or opportunities, (2) Whether they're already a customer, (3) Any existing contact records. If it finds a match, the person is skipped to prevent duplicate outreach.

04
Step 4

Find Email Addresses

Finds the right person's email at the company using tools like Clay, Apollo, or ZoomInfo.

Clay, Apollo, ZoomInfo, or Amplemarket

How It Works

Connects to a data provider and searches for people at the company who match your criteria (like "VP of Sales" or "Head of Marketing"). Returns verified email addresses, phone numbers, and information about the company. Can try multiple data sources to get the best results.

05
Step 5

Write Custom Email

AI researches the company and writes a personalized 3-sentence email explaining how you can help.

Claude, GPT-4, or DeepSeek

How It Works

AI looks up information about the company and their industry, then writes a 3-sentence email that: (1) Mentions a specific problem they likely have, (2) Explains why it's costing them time or money, (3) Suggests how you can help with a clear next step.

06
Step 6

Choose: Auto-Send or Approve First

Decides whether to send automatically or ask for your approval in Slack first.

Decision point

How It Works

Checks your settings to see if this campaign should auto-send or require approval. If approval is needed, it sends a message to Slack. If auto-send is on, it goes straight to adding the person to your CRM and email sequence.

07
Step 7

Add to Your CRM

Creates or updates the contact in your CRM with their info and notes that outreach is happening.

HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, or Pipedrive

How It Works

Creates a new contact (or updates an existing one) in your CRM with: their email and phone number, job title and company, notes from the AI research, and a status showing they're in an active outreach campaign.

08
Step 8

Add to Email Sequence

Enrolls them in your email sequence with their personalized message and starts sending.

Smartlead, Instantly, Lemlist, or HubSpot

How It Works

Connects to your email sending tool and adds this person to the campaign you specified. It includes their name, company, and the personalized message AI wrote. The email sequence starts automatically and tracks responses back to your CRM.

09
Approval Path

Send Slack Approval

Sends you a message in Slack with the person's details and email preview. Click to approve or skip.

Slack message

How It Works

Sends a formatted message to your Slack channel showing: the person's name and company, what AI found during research, a preview of the email that will be sent, and buttons to Approve (which continues to step 7) or Reject (which stops here).
